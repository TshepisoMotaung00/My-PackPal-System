# 🎒 My-PackPal-System
### PackPal Business System - Mahikeng, North West

A Python system I built to run my small business selling quality bags and bottles in Mahikeng, North West.

## ✨ What It Does
- **View Stock:** Lunch Bags (Black, Brown, Blue, Grey) & Flask Bottles (Grey, Gold, Maroon, Blue) with low-stock alerts
- **Record Sale:** Capture customer name, contact, location + auto-generate professional receipt (.txt)
- **Place Order (On The Road):** Save orders when stock is low while I'm out selling
- **View & Deliver Orders:** Track pending orders and convert to sales
- **Sales & Profit Report:** Total sales, profit per item, credit tracking with month-end due dates
